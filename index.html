Scoring notes

Each item scores 0 (absent), 1, or 2 points as defined in Table 2. Maximum total = 32 (Gray matter 12 + White matter/cortex 12 + Cerebellum 4 + Additional 4). Intraventricular and subdural hemorrhage cap at 1 point.

Adapted from Weeke L, et al. A novel magnetic resonance imaging score predicts neurodevelopmental outcome after perinatal asphyxia and therapeutic hypothermia. J Pediatr 2018;192:33-40, as modified in the Canadian consensus below.
